# Founding Plan — Virtual Commerce Office

## Purpose
A second virtual office, mirroring the operating model of the Virtual IT
Office, purpose-built for an Amazon FBA + DTC e-commerce business: find
what to sell, source it from China, ship it, warehouse it, list it on
Amazon, sell it on Shopify, and keep it all legal and profitable.

## Organization
14 seats in 7 divisions, led by Mercer (Chief Operator) — the one person
who handles everything and reports to Naman.

- **Leadership:** Mercer — intake, dispatch, War Room facilitation,
  reporting, final sign-off.
- **Sourcing & Supply Chain:** China Sourcing Expert (negotiation,
  factories, terms), Vendor Picker (on-the-ground China agent: factory
  visits, QC).
- **Logistics & Warehousing:** Shipping Expert (freight + forwarder
  consultancy), Warehouse Expert (3PLs, FBA prep, storage economics).
- **Marketplaces:** Amazon FBA Specialist (account, shipments, health),
  Amazon Services Expert (listings, PPC, Brand Registry), Product
  Suggester (what to sell, when — trends and seasonality).
- **Digital Storefront:** Website Expert, Shopify Expert, UI Designer
  (outstanding UI bar).
- **Legal & Finance:** International Trade Lawyer, Finance & Tax Counsel.
- **Intelligence:** Knowledge Wizard — answers any question via
  whole-internet research, returns the simplest no-extra-steps solution,
  and spawns subagents to continue/complete the job.

## Operating model
Same as the IT office: playbooks run as one subagent per step; War Room
before any launch; memory in `/memory/` wings; decisions logged;
Mercer reports to the owner.

## Non-goals (v1)
No real-money transactions by agents, no impersonation of the owner to
suppliers, no binding legal/tax advice without licensed counsel review.
