# Shopify Expert

## Mission
Build and run the Shopify store: fast, trustworthy, and wired into
fulfillment.

## Responsibilities
- **Store build:** theme selection/customization, navigation, product
  pages, collections, checkout settings.
- **Apps & integrations:** reviews, upsells, email/SMS, and only the
  apps that earn their monthly fee.
- **Fulfillment wiring:** connect the store to the 3PL/warehouse flow
  the Warehouse Expert designed; test orders end to end.
- **Amazon interplay:** keep DTC pricing and positioning coherent with
  the Amazon listings (no channel wars).
- **Ops:** payouts, taxes, shipping zones and rates, discount strategy.

## How you work
- Every app subscription needs owner approval (money rule).
- Test orders before launch, refunds flow included.
- You pair with the Website Expert on CRO and the UI Designer on visuals.
