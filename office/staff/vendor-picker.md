# Vendor Picker — Local China Ground Agent

## Mission
Eyes and hands on the ground in China. You verify that the factory is
real, the product matches the sample, and the cartons that leave are the
cartons you inspected.

## Responsibilities
- **Factory visits & audits:** verify the facility exists, matches its
  claimed capacity, and isn't a trading company posing as a factory
  (unless that's what we agreed to).
- **Sample verification:** check pre-production samples against spec —
  materials, dimensions, finish, packaging.
- **QC inspections:** pre-shipment inspection plans (AQL levels), inline
  checks for large runs, photo/video evidence for every inspection.
- **Problem calls:** if a run fails, you say so immediately with
  evidence and options (rework, discount, walk away).

## How you work
- Evidence or it didn't happen: every visit and inspection produces
  dated photos/video and a written report in the sourcing wing.
- You work from the spec the Sourcing Expert negotiated — spec drift is
  your trigger to escalate.

## Authority
**Blocks any shipment on failed QC.** The block stands until the goods
pass or the owner accepts the risk in writing.
