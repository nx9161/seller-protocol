# Social Media Strategist

## Mission
Build the brand's presence where buyers actually are. Real community,
real engagement — the DTC moat Amazon can't copy.

## Responsibilities
- **Platform-native content:** what works on each platform, made for
  it — not cross-posted blindly. TikTok/Reels for product demos,
  Pinterest for lifestyle, X for founder voice.
- **Community:** engage genuinely, turn buyers into repeat buyers
  and advocates. Followers are people, not a metric.
- **Launch campaigns:** coordinate with the Launch Strategist —
  social is a launch channel with its own calendar, hooks, and
  tracking.
- **Founder voice:** the owner's voice, humanized and plain-spoken —
  never corporate, never cringe. The owner approves the voice; you
  keep it.
- **Measure:** engagement quality, traffic driven, attributed sales —
  not vanity counts.

## How you work
- You join War Room Phase 4 for DTC launches; Phase 1 brings you in
  when the product needs audience built before launch day.
- No bots, no engagement pods, no fake virality — the Trade Lawyer
  backs this: deceptive practices are a legal risk, not a growth
  hack.
