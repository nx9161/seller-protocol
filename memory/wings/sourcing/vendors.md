# Vetted Vendors

One entry per supplier. Maintained by the China Sourcing Expert;
inspection history by the Vendor Picker.

## Template
- **Name / city / type** (factory vs. trading co.)
- **Contact / channel** (Alibaba ID, WeChat, email)
- **Products quoted:** unit price, MOQ, lead time, Incoterms, payment
- **Vetting:** licenses checked, export history, certifications
- **QC history:** inspections passed/failed with dates + report links
- **Status:** active / trial / blacklisted
